Date calculator for Québec — free trilingual tool

CalculDates.ca is a free Québec-based tool that helps parents, workers, students, managers and professionals quickly calculate important dates: administrative deadlines, statutory holidays, legal timelines, pregnancy weeks, shared-custody calendars, work rotations and payroll periods. The site is entirely free, no signup required, and available in French, English and Spanish.

Whether you need to plan your return to work after parental leave, check the deadline to challenge a dismissal, organize your children's custody schedule or simply add 90 days to a purchase date to check a warranty, this calculator gives you the answer in seconds — with the day of the week, the number of business days and the corresponding civil week.

How to use the date calculator

  1. Choose the direction of calculation with the toggle at the top: "Start date → Deadline" (forward calculation) or "Deadline → Start date" (backward calculation, useful to know when to send a notice).
  2. Select the reference date using the date picker. The day of the week is displayed automatically to confirm your choice.
  3. Enter the quantity and unit of time: days, weeks, months or years. For a 45-day deadline, for example, select "45" and "Day(s)".
  4. Read the result: the calculated date, the corresponding day of the week, plus complementary information (total days, full weeks, full months, start of the civil week).

You can also use the common Québec scenarios shown below the calculator: a single click fills in the right values for common deadlines like maternity leave (QPIP), lease termination notice, a Labour Standards complaint or warranty expiry.

Why a date calculator is useful in everyday life

Many administrative or legal situations in Québec depend on a precise deadline expressed in days, weeks or months. A few concrete examples:

  • Residential lease termination notice: under the Tribunal administratif du logement, the tenant must send a non-renewal notice between 3 and 6 months before the end of the lease (depending on the lease length). Our tool calculates the deadline backward from the lease end date.
  • Wrongful dismissal complaint: the Act respecting labour standards sets a 45-day deadline from dismissal to file a complaint with the CNESST. Calculate the deadline so you don't miss it.
  • Québec Parental Insurance Plan (QPIP) periods: maternity leave (15 or 18 weeks depending on the plan), paternity leave (3 or 5 weeks), parental leave (25 or 32 shareable weeks). Visualize the exact dates based on when you plan to start.
  • Legal and contractual warranty: 1 year for apparent defects, up to 5 years for hidden defects under Québec law. Add the duration to the purchase date to find the deadline.
  • Civil prescription period: 3 years for most civil liability claims (article 2925 of the Civil Code of Québec). Essential to know if there's still time to sue.
  • Tax periods and filings: align your period-end and tax-deadline dates with your fiscal year.
💡 Tip: deadlines counted in days generally begin the day after the triggering event (article 83 of the Code of Civil Procedure of Québec). If the last day falls on a Saturday, Sunday or statutory holiday, it is postponed to the next business day. For sensitive situations, always verify the date with a lawyer or qualified advisor.

Frequently asked questions

Is the date calculator reliable?

The tool uses standard date functions of the Gregorian calendar (the official civil calendar of Québec and Canada). It accounts for leap years, variable month lengths and year transitions. For legal or administrative deadlines, however, we recommend confirming the date with the official source or a professional — some deadlines have specific rules (counting from the next day, postponement to the next business day, etc.).

Is my data saved?

No. All operations happen directly in your browser, with no data sent to a server. No date and no personal information is stored by CalculDates.ca. Only your language preference is saved locally (in localStorage) so you don't have to choose it again on each visit.

Can I use this calculator outside Québec?

Yes — date operations work anywhere. However, the proposed scenarios (lease notice, QPIP leaves, Labour Standards complaints, etc.) are based on Québec legislation. For other jurisdictions, use the free calculator and verify the applicable deadlines with local authorities.

How do I calculate a date backward?

Activate the "Deadline → Start date" toggle at the top of the calculator. Then enter the deadline date and the duration to subtract. Example: to know when to send a 3-month notice before your lease ends on June 30, 2026, select backward mode, date 2026-06-30, and "3 Months" — the tool will give you the deadline to send the notice.

How do I count business days?

For custody, rotation or payroll calendars, our specialized tools account for business days, weekends and statutory holidays. For an ad-hoc business-day calculation, use the Statutory holidays page to export the year's holidays, then subtract them manually from your period.

Why are there ads on the site?

CalculDates.ca is entirely free. To cover hosting and development costs, the site displays a few ads (Google AdSense) and participates in affiliate programs (Amazon Associates, Le CFO masqué). None of these ads follow you outside the site, and our privacy policy details exactly which data is shared.
