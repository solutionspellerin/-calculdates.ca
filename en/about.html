About CalculDates.ca

CalculDates.ca brings together free tools to work out the dates that matter in Québec: legal and administrative deadlines, shared custody calendars, work rotations, pregnancy weeks, payroll calendars and public holidays.

Who is behind CalculDates.ca

CalculDates.ca is designed and maintained by Marie-Pier Pellerin, founder of Solutions Pellerin, a company based in Val-des-Sources (Québec) that specializes in process optimization and business intelligence. Her work involves helping organizations automate their processes and build dashboards (Zoho, Power BI, Excel), and designing training, including Lean Six Sigma, Excel and Power BI courses.

CalculDates.ca applies the same approach to everyday questions: start from a precise rule — a legal deadline, a custody schedule, a work rotation, a payroll calendar — and turn it into a calculation that is clear, verifiable and free.

Our tools

  • 📆 Date calculator

    Add or subtract days, weeks, months or years, forwards or backwards, with ready-made Québec scenarios.

  • 📅 Shared custody

    Visual 7/7, 2-2-3, 3-2-2-3, 2-2-5-5, 5/2 or weekend calendar, exportable to PDF and your calendar app.

  • 🔁 Work rotation

    2/2, 14/14, 7/7 or custom schedules for camp work, construction and atypical shifts.

  • 🤰 Pregnancy (CNESST)

    Pregnancy weeks and key points of the Safe Maternity program, with QPIP (RQAP) milestones.

  • 💸 Pay and fiscal

    Pay periods, submission and deposit dates, fiscal year and 4-4-5 periods.

  • 🎉 Public holidays

    Your organization's holiday list, with suggestions by country and province, exportable to PDF, Excel and .ics.

  • 📚 Practical guide

    The rules behind the calculations: QPIP, CNESST, labour standards, residential leases, custody and rotations.

How our calculations are built

  • Every tool starts from a published rule (statute, regulation or official program), cited on the relevant page.
  • Calculations run entirely in your browser: the dates and information you enter are neither stored nor transmitted by our calculators.
  • Assumptions and limits are shown next to the results (for example, a deadline falling on a public holiday or a more generous collective agreement).
  • Results are meant for planning and checking; for an important decision, confirm them with the agency concerned or a professional.

Updates and corrections

Rules change: public plan parameters, rates, public holidays, laws. We review the tools when those rules change. If you spot an error or a case that is handled poorly, write to info@calculdates.ca with the values you entered and the result you expected: every report is checked and corrected where needed.
